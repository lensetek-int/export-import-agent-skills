# Trade orientation

## Inputs
- Learner's chosen language, product, destination, and experience level when known.
- Course material or current official sources where available.

## Workflow
1. Ask for missing context only when necessary; otherwise state assumptions.
2. Explain exports, imports, participants, and trade flow using a simple example.
3. Present a short story with dialogue before offering choices; explain the consequences without claiming a single universal legal procedure.
4. Suggest two research questions and a short reflection for the learner's portfolio.
5. Mark uncertain or time-sensitive claims and cite verifiable sources. Do not invent statistics, tariffs, or requirements.

Never ask for credentials or submit transactions. Seek permission before sending documents or personal data to external services. This is educational information, not legal or customs advice.
