# Trade research

## Inputs
- Product, origin, potential destination, and intended audience.

## Workflow
1. Clarify scope and list missing information.
2. Propose research questions covering demand, competition, logistics, and relevant requirements.
3. If browsing is available and authorized, locate relevant official or primary sources and record links, publication or access dates, and limitations.
4. Separate verified findings, assumptions, and unresolved questions; never fabricate data, HS classifications, or regulatory conclusions.
5. Help the learner prepare a concise evidence table and portfolio reflection in the requested language.

Do not transmit private business information without permission. Check current rules with qualified professionals and competent authorities.
